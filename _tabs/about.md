## Junseok Kim

I am a Cellular Protocol Firmware Engineer at Samsung Electronics, working on 5G NR/LTE protocol modem firmware for commercial mobile devices. My main interests include mobile systems, wireless network protocols, modem performance optimization, and hardware-software co-design.

My work sits at the intersection of protocol design and embedded system implementation. I have worked on improving throughput, voice quality, power efficiency, CPU workload, and real-time stability in cellular modem systems, contributing to features used in flagship devices such as Galaxy and Pixel series. I especially enjoy debugging complex cross-layer problems and turning system-level insights into practical firmware improvements.

Before joining industry, I received my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Seoul National University, where I studied wireless networks, random access, and PHY/MAC system-level modeling. This background continues to shape how I approach engineering problems: from first principles, across layers, and with attention to both algorithmic design and real-world constraints.

This website is where I share technical notes and long-form posts on cellular systems, RAN protocols, Open-RAN, Sionna, and other hands-on engineering topics.
